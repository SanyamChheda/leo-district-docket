District GST Vision | LY 2026-27

WHAT DOES "PASSION IGNITES PURPOSE" MEAN?

Passion is the driving force behind every act of service. It inspires us to step forward, take initiative, and make a difference within our communities.

Purpose gives direction to that passion. It helps us identify genuine needs, create meaningful solutions, and ensure that our efforts result in lasting impact.

When passion is guided by purpose, service evolves from an activity into an impact.

OUR APPROACH TO SERVICE

Leo District 3231 A2 believes in service that is:

● Meaningful

● Beneficiary-Centric

● Hands-On

● Innovative

● Impact-Driven

● Sustainable

Who Is A Club GST?

WHO IS A CLUB GST?

A Club Global Service Team (GST) Officer is much more than an activity coordinator.

A Club GST is a service leader, planner, collaborator, and impact creator who helps transform ideas into meaningful service initiatives. They serve as the bridge between community needs and the club's ability to create positive change.

Most importantly, a Club GST plays a key role in building a culture where service becomes a way of life rather than a responsibility assigned to a single individual.

YOUR CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

As a Club GST, you are expected to:

● Plan and execute meaningful service activities throughout the year.

● Identify genuine community needs and beneficiaries before planning projects.

● Encourage active participation of club members in service activities.

● Conduct and promote at least one hands-on service activity every month.

● Try to cover all 8 Global Service Areas during the Leoistic Year.

● Maintain service records, beneficiary records, and impact data.

● Ensure timely and accurate reporting of all service activities.

● Support and promote participation in district-wide service initiatives.

● Work closely with the Club President, Secretary, Board of Directors, and fellow members to strengthen the club's service efforts.

BEYOND THE ROLE: WHAT MAKES AN EXCEPTIONAL GST?

An exceptional Club GST goes beyond conducting activities. They:

● Inspire others to serve.

● Focus on impact rather than activity counts.

● Promote hands-on service and beneficiary engagement.

● Encourage innovation and creative problem-solving.

● Build partnerships with NGOs, institutions, Lions Clubs, and other Leo Clubs.

● Explore collaboration and twinning opportunities.

● Create sustainable and recurring service initiatives.

● Continuously look for ways to improve the quality and reach of their club's service.

THINK IMPACT, NOT ACTIVITIES

Before planning any project, ask yourself:

● What community need are we addressing?

● Who are the beneficiaries?

● What impact do we hope to create?

● How will we measure success?

● Can this initiative create long-term value?

Meaningful service is not measured by the number of activities conducted, but by the lives positively impacted through them.

BUILDING A CULTURE OF SERVICE

One of the most important responsibilities of a Club GST is to ensure that service does not become a compulsion or simply another item on a checklist. Instead, service should become a culture within the club.

Every Leo should feel encouraged to participate, contribute, and experience service firsthand. When service becomes a shared responsibility and a shared passion, clubs create stronger leaders, stronger communities, and greater impact.

A successful GST does not simply organize service - they inspire others to serve.

GST TAKEAWAY

"A Club GST is successful when they inspire their club to serve with purpose, involve Leos through meaningful hands-on experiences, create lasting impact within the community, and ensure that service becomes a culture within the club rather than a responsibility assigned to a single individual."

Service Planning & The 8 Lions Focus Areas

PLANNING AN IMPACTFUL SERVICE ACTIVITY

Every successful service project begins with proper planning. Before conducting any activity, club GSTs are encouraged to follow this simple framework:

1. IDENTIFY THE NEED

● What community need are we addressing?

● Is this a genuine and relevant issue?

2. IDENTIFY THE BENEFICIARIES

● Who will benefit from this activity?

● How will the project positively impact them?

3. PLAN THE ACTIVITY

● What resources are required?

● What is the budget?

● Who will be responsible for execution?

4. ENGAGE LEOS

● How can members actively participate?

● Can the activity be made more hands-on and engaging?

5. MEASURE & REPORT IMPACT

● What impact was created?

● How many beneficiaries were served?

● What outcomes were achieved?

● How will the activity be documented and reported?

GST RESOURCE HUB

To support clubs in planning and executing meaningful service initiatives, the following resources are highly recommended:

● LIONS SERVICE PROJECT PLANNER - A comprehensive planning tool developed by Lions Clubs International to help clubs define project objectives, allocate responsibilities, plan resources and budgets, manage volunteers, measure project outcomes, and evaluate impact and improve future activities.

● LIONS INTERNATIONAL SERVICE TOOLKIT - The Service Toolkit provides access to guides, templates, project ideas, campaign resources, and planning tools that can help clubs create more impactful service initiatives.

THE 8 LIONS FOCUS AREAS

Lions Clubs International has identified eight key service areas that address some of the most pressing needs across communities worldwide. Club GSTs are encouraged to try and cover all eight focus areas during the Leoistic Year while prioritizing genuine community needs and meaningful impact.

● HUNGER - Addressing food insecurity and supporting nutrition-related initiatives.

● ENVIRONMENT - Protecting and preserving the environment through sustainable actions.

● DIABETES - Promoting awareness, prevention, and management of diabetes.

● VISION - Supporting eye health and helping prevent avoidable blindness.

● CHILDHOOD CANCER - Supporting children and families affected by childhood cancer.

● HUMANITARIAN EFFORTS - Addressing community needs through service and support initiatives.

● DISASTER RELIEF - Providing assistance and support to communities affected by disasters.

● YOUTH - Empowering young people through leadership, education, and development opportunities.

Reporting & Documentation

WHY REPORTING MATTERS

Reporting is much more than an administrative requirement. It helps us:

● Measure the impact created through service.

● Understand community needs and beneficiary outreach.

● Track Leo participation and engagement.

● Document service initiatives for future reference.

● Identify successful projects and best practices.

● Showcase the collective impact created across the district.

Reporting allows us to transform service activities into measurable stories of impact.

GST REPORTING PROCESS

For Leoistic Year 2026-27, all service activities are required to be reported through the District GST Reporting System (apart from the Secretarial Team's reporting requirements).

Each activity should be reported within 48 hours of its completion through the designated Google Form.

The activity may be reported by:

● The Club GST

● The Club President

● The Club Vice President

A separate form must be submitted for each individual activity. This reporting system will help the District GST Team better understand activity planning, execution, beneficiary outreach, Leo participation, challenges faced, and overall impact measurement across the district.

WHAT SHOULD BE REPORTED?

The reporting form may include details such as:

● Activity Name

● Activity Date

● Venue / Location

● Lions Focus Area

● Number of Beneficiaries

● Number of Leos Participated

● Leo Hours

● Funds Raised

● Funds Utilized

● Activity Chairperson

● Twinning / Collaboration Details

● Beneficiary Identification Process

● Activity Description

● Impact Created

● Challenges or obstacles faced while conceptualizing the activity

● Challenges or obstacles faced during activity execution

● Key learnings and observations

● Supporting Photographs & Videos

Additional information may be requested based on the nature of the activity and reporting requirements.

CAPTURING IMPACT

Good documentation strengthens good service. While capturing photographs and videos, clubs are encouraged to:

● Take clear and high-quality photographs.

● Capture active Leo participation.

● Highlight beneficiary engagement wherever appropriate.

● Include photographs that reflect the true impact of the project.

● Capture before-and-after photographs whenever relevant.

● Ensure photographs are respectful and maintain beneficiary dignity.

● Upload only relevant and meaningful media.

Avoid:

● Blurry photographs.

● Excessive group photographs.

● Staged or artificial photographs.

● Photographs that fail to showcase the activity or its impact.

COMMON REPORTING MISTAKES TO AVOID

Some of the most common reporting errors include:

● Late submission of reports.

● Incorrect beneficiary counts.

● Missing or incomplete activity descriptions.

● Failure to explain the impact created.

● Selecting the wrong Lions Focus Area.

● Missing photographs or supporting documentation.

● Incomplete financial information.

● Reporting without proper verification of details.

Taking a few extra minutes to review your report before submission can significantly improve reporting quality and accuracy.

REPORT IMPACT, NOT JUST ACTIVITIES

A good report tells us what happened. A great report tells us why it mattered.

When reporting an activity, focus on:

● The problem that was addressed.

● The beneficiaries who were impacted.

● The outcomes that were achieved.

● The challenges that were overcome.

● The lessons learned.

● The value created for the community.

Remember, numbers tell a story - but impact gives those numbers meaning.

GST TAKEAWAY

"Reporting is not about documenting what was done; it is about demonstrating the difference that was made." Document accurately. Report promptly. Measure impact meaningfully.

GST Monthly Success Scorecard

WHY USE THIS SCORECARD?

Being a successful Club GST is not just about conducting activities - it is about creating impact, engaging Leos, maintaining consistency, and strengthening your club's service culture. At the end of every month, take a few minutes to review your progress using this self-assessment scorecard.

SERVICE PLANNING

● Planned service activities for the month.

● Identified genuine beneficiaries and community needs.

● Considered innovative or sustainable service opportunities.

SERVICE EXECUTION

● Conducted at least one service activity.

● Conducted at least one hands-on service activity.

● Encouraged active Leo participation.

● Ensured activities addressed genuine community needs.

IMPACT & DOCUMENTATION

● Tracked beneficiaries served.

● Measured and documented impact created.

● Maintained service records and activity documentation.

● Captured meaningful photographs and activity evidence.

REPORTING

● Submitted all GST reports within 48 hours of activity completion.

● Verified that reporting information was complete and accurate.

● Documented impact, outcomes, and learnings effectively.

DISTRICT ENGAGEMENT

● Stayed updated with district GST announcements.

● Participated in district initiatives and opportunities wherever applicable.

● Supported district campaigns and service efforts.

COLLABORATION & GROWTH

● Explored collaboration or twinning opportunities.

● Connected with other clubs, organizations, or partners where possible.

● Shared ideas, best practices, or innovative approaches.

LOOKING AHEAD

● Planned activities for the upcoming month.

● Identified upcoming service opportunities, observances, or initiatives.

● Set clear service goals for the next month.

GST MONTHLY SCORE

Count the number of boxes checked:

● 18-21 Checks - Outstanding Month: You are actively leading service, creating impact, and strengthening your club's service culture.

● 14-17 Checks - Strong Progress: You are on the right track. Continue building consistency and looking for opportunities to grow.

● 10-13 Checks - Good Foundation: Your club is making progress, but there are areas where greater engagement and planning can improve results.

● Below 10 Checks - Opportunity for Improvement: Review the checklist, identify gaps, and work with your club and the District GST Team to strengthen your service efforts.
